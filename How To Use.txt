AutoWriter — Human-like Typing Automation
==========================================

Double-click AutoWriter.exe to launch. No installation needed.


HOW TO USE
----------

1. Type or paste the text you want to auto-type into the text box.

2. Pick your settings (see Features below).

3. Click "Start Typing" — a countdown begins (default 5 seconds).

4. During the countdown, switch to your browser and click the exact
   field or text box you want to type into.

5. AutoWriter will start typing your text automatically.

6. Click "Stop" at any time to cancel.
   Or move your mouse to the top-left corner of the screen to abort.

7. Closing the window sends AutoWriter to the system tray.
   Right-click the tray icon to show or quit the app.


FEATURES
--------

Text Editor
  Type freely. Select text and click B / I / U / H1 / H2 in the
  toolbar to apply formatting. Undo and Redo are supported.

Speed Preset
  Slow, Medium, Fast, Very Fast — controls how quickly characters
  are typed.

Rhythm Mode
  Natural  — realistic random variation, like a real person typing.
  Burst    — types in fast bursts with pauses in between.
  Fatigue  — starts at normal speed and gradually slows down.
  Steady   — consistent, even pace throughout.

Typo + Self-Correct
  Occasionally mispresses an adjacent key then catches and fixes it,
  just like a human would.

Anti-Detection (Undetect Mode)
  Uses Windows low-level input (Win32 SendInput) instead of standard
  automation — harder for websites to detect as a bot. Also adds
  random mouse micro-movement and thinking pauses when enabled.

WPM Target
  Set a specific words-per-minute target. Overrides the speed preset
  when set above 0.

Countdown
  How many seconds you have to switch to your browser after clicking
  Start. Adjustable from 3 to 15 seconds.

Minimize to Tray
  Click the "Tray" button or close the window — the app keeps running
  silently in the system tray. Click the tray icon to bring it back.
